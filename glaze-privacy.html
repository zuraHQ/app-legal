Glaze – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2, 2026

Glaze (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Glaze.

1. Information We Collect

  • Usage Data: We automatically collect information about how you interact with the app, including keystroke suggestions, feature usage, and app performance.
  • Device Data: We may collect device type, operating system, and version for analytics and app optimization.
  • AI Interaction Data: Your typed input may be processed by our AI to provide keyboard suggestions. This data is used only to improve app performance and user experience.

Note: We do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide AI-powered keyboard suggestions.
  • To analyze app performance and improve features.
  • To detect bugs and crashes for better app stability.

3. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.
  • We may share anonymous usage data with analytics providers to improve Glaze.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no system is completely secure, and use of the app is at your own risk.

5. Children’s Privacy

Glaze is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting in the app.
